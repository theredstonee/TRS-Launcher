The TRS Launcher runs on your computer. It has **no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting or advertising**. It only
connects to a service when that is needed for something you asked it to do.

::note
This page is a short overview. The full privacy policy is on the website:
[trs-launcher.theredstonee.de/privacy](https://trs-launcher.theredstonee.de/privacy).
::

## Who the launcher talks to

| Service | When |
|---|---|
| Microsoft, Xbox Live, Minecraft services | Signing in and starting the game |
| Mojang (game files, Java, version data, news) | Installing or starting a version; news on the start page |
| Fabric, Quilt, Forge, NeoForge | Installing a mod loader |
| Modrinth | Browsing, installing or updating content; trending projects in the news |
| CurseForge | Only when you use CurseForge content |
| Minecraft servers in your server list | Showing player count and ping |
| GitHub | Launcher and TRS Client updates, release news; FFmpeg once when you turn on clips (Windows) |
| mclo.gs | Only when you press **Share log** and confirm |
| Discord app on your PC (local, no internet) | While **Show Discord status** is on |
| TRS server | Only after you turned on the TRS services |

## What stays on your PC

- **Your Microsoft sign-in**: tokens are stored encrypted – on Windows with DPAPI, on Linux with a key from the system
  keyring. The launcher never sees your password.
- **Crash analysis**: the [crash helper](/en/help/crash-helper) works only on your PC.
- **Imports** from other launchers: their files are only read on your PC; their sign-in data is never read.
- **Clips and recordings** never leave your PC.

## TRS services (optional)

Capes, friends, online status, chat, synced skins and settings, achievements and more come from the TRS server. They
are **off until you agree** in the launcher. The TRS server never sees your password or your Minecraft access token –
it checks your account the same way a Minecraft server does.

You decide in **Settings → Privacy**:

- **Use TRS services**: off = the launcher sends nothing to the TRS server, and the TRS Client doesn't use it either.
- **Online status**, **TRS badge**, **cape visible to others**, **share server** and **sync** can be switched
  separately.
- **Delete all TRS data** deletes your TRS account and its data on the server at once. The skins and presets on your PC
  stay.

## Your settings at a glance

| Setting | Default | Where |
|---|---|---|
| TRS services | off until you agree | Settings → Privacy |
| Allow sharing logs | on (uploads only when you confirm) | Settings → Privacy |
| Show Discord status | on | Settings → Privacy |
| Clips & recording | off | Settings → Clips & recording |
