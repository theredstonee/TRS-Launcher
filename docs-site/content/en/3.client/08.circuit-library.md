The **Circuit Library** is a page in the category **Redstone** with ready-made circuits: logic gates, repeater chain, torch tower, clocks, memory (RS latch, T flip-flops, D latch), pulse circuits, a 2×2 piston door and a hidden staircase, farm basics (sugar cane, item filter, automatic furnace, item elevator) and displays.

::note
The circuit library works from Minecraft 1.8.9 on (not on 1.7.10 and 1.13.2). The circuits come from the TRS server, so new ones appear without a mod update. The library checks for changes once per start and keeps a copy for offline play – before the very first download it needs internet.
::

![The circuit library with a circuit's 3D preview and material list](/shots/client/circuits-library.png)

## Find a circuit

Open the TRS menu → **Redstone** → **Circuit Library** → **Open library**. You can also bind the key **Open the library** on the module page (unbound by default).

- **Search** and categories help you find the right circuit, **My version** hides the ones your Minecraft version can't build.
- Each circuit has an explanation, difficulty, size, the Minecraft version it needs and whether it runs reliably on servers.
- The **Materials** list shows how many of each block you already have in your inventory.
- The 3D preview can be turned and viewed layer by layer.

## Show a circuit as a template

::steps
### Place the template
Click **Show as template here**. The template follows your view – press :kbd{value="R"} to rotate it and :kbd{value="Enter"} to confirm the position.

### Build it block by block
Ghost blocks show what goes where:
- **green** – the block is right,
- **red** – the block is wrong (with the reason),
- **grey** – the block is still missing.

A progress bar shows "32/40 blocks".

### Work layer by layer
:kbd{value="↑"} and :kbd{value="↓"} switch to the next or previous layer, :kbd{value="H"} hides or shows the template.

### Remove it
When you are done, click **Remove template** in the library or on the module page.
::

![Ghost blocks of a template in the world, green, red and grey](/shots/client/circuits-ghost.png)

The template stays in the world or on the server when you reconnect. The button **Mirrored** next to **Show as template here** flips the circuit before you place it.

::tip
The template is display only, like a schematic preview: nothing is built for you and nothing is sent to the server – fine on servers with fair-play rules or anti-cheat.
::

### Settings

Block names next to missing and wrong blocks, ghost block opacity, **Only outline correct blocks** and the keys for rotating, confirming, layers and hiding.

## Submit your own circuit

You need to be signed in with the TRS online features.

::steps
### Mark the area
Click **Submit your own circuit** → **Mark area**. Look at one corner and confirm, then at the opposite corner. The area can be up to 16×16×16 blocks. Only the blocks are read – no chest contents.

### Describe it
Give it a **Name**, a **Category** and a **Short description**.

### Submit
Click **Submit**. The TRS team checks every submission before it appears in everyone's library. Only submit your own designs.
::

**My submissions** shows whether each circuit is waiting for review, approved or rejected (with the reason).
