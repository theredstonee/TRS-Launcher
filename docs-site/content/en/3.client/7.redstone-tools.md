The category **Redstone** of the TRS menu has three tools for building circuits. The **Redstone** module pack under **Presets** turns on everything you need, with a matching HUD layout.

## Signal Strength

![Signal Strength HUD next to a comparator](/shots/client/redstone-signal.png)

Look at dust, repeaters, comparators, pistons, lamps, levers and more. The HUD element shows:

- the block name and its **signal strength** (0 – 15) with a signal bar,
- the **delay** of a repeater (and whether it is locked),
- the **mode** of a comparator (compare or subtract) and its output,
- whether a piston is extended or retracted,
- the comparator output of containers.

::tip
Minecraft only tells the client what is inside a chest or hopper while it is open. The TRS Client remembers the content from the last time you opened it and marks such values with "(last opened)".
::

Settings: **Block name**, **Signal bar**, **Details (delay, mode, state)**. On by default.

## Signal Overlay

Shows the signal strength as a number above every piece of redstone dust around you – from grey (0) to bright red (15). Switch it on and off with its key:

::tabs
:::tabs-item{label="1.9 and newer"}
:kbd{value="F6"} – **Toggle signal overlay**
:::
:::tabs-item{label="1.7.10 and 1.8.9"}
:kbd{value="F8"} – F6 is used by Minecraft's stream keys in these versions.
:::
::

![Signal overlay with numbers above the redstone dust](/shots/client/redstone-overlay.png)

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Radius (blocks) | 8 (4 – 16) |
| Only dust in sight | on |
| Also label 0 | on |

## Clock Meter

Measures how fast the redstone part you look at switches: frequency in **Hz**, period and pulse length in redstone ticks (rt) and game ticks (gt), plus a small oscilloscope. It only appears while a part is actually switching.

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Measuring window (s) | 5 |
| Oscilloscope | on |
| Keep measuring after looking away | on |

## Version notes

::note
The redstone tools work in every version from 1.7.10 to 26.3. The ready-made circuits are on the next page: [Circuit library](/en/client/circuit-library).
::
