## Minimap

![Round minimap with compass ring, waypoints on the edge and coordinates below](/shots/client/minimap.png)

The minimap is off by default. Turn it on in the TRS menu under **World → Minimap**; move and resize it in the HUD editor.

It shows real block colours (taken from the textures of your resource pack), biome-tinted grass, leaves and water, soft relief shading and water that gets darker with depth. Underground and in the Nether it switches to a **cave view** on its own, and under a roof it shows the **inside** of the building instead of the roof.

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | Round (or Square) |
| Size (pixels) | 112 (64 – 256) |
| Zoom | Normal (Very far, Far, Normal, Near, Very close) |
| Rotate with view | on |
| Auto zoom: further out when fast / closer indoors and in caves | on / on |
| Map opacity | 100 % |
| Map colors | Block textures (or Vanilla map colors) |
| Cave view | Automatic (or Off) |
| Hide roofs (show the inside of buildings) | on |
| Show waypoints / waypoints out of view at the map edge | on / on |
| Show last death point | on |
| Show players / highlight TRS friends | on / on |
| Show hostile mobs / show animals | off / off |
| Creatures as | Heads (or Symbols) |
| Compass directions, coordinates and biome below the map | on |
| Time of day below the map | off |

Waypoints outside the map sit as small coloured boxes with their first letter on the ring and point the way.

## Fair Play

**Fair Play** turns off everything a map could use to cheat, for the minimap and the world map together:

- no cave view and no indoor view,
- players and mobs only show up when you could actually see them.

::steps
### Switch it on yourself
**Minimap → Fair Play (maps show only what you could see)**, or the Fair Play switch on the world map page.

### Or let the server decide
Servers that ask map mods for fair play (the common Xaero codes in chat or the server description) are respected automatically. A server can also turn the minimap off completely – the minimap page then tells you.
::

## Waypoints

::steps
### Create a waypoint
Press :kbd{value="B"}, pick a name and a colour, and save. It is created where you stand.

### Manage your waypoints
Press :kbd{value="N"} or click **Manage waypoints** on the **Waypoints** module page: edit, hide or delete.

### Find your way
In the world, each waypoint shows a **light beam** and its **distance**. After you die, a **death point** is set automatically.
::

Waypoints are saved per world or server on your PC. **Only up to (blocks, 0 = always)** hides far waypoints. The keys B and N are set on the module page (they are not in the Minecraft controls).

::tip
Coordinates that someone writes in the Minecraft chat (like `x: 100 y: 64 z: -20` or `100 64 -20`) are underlined – one click saves them as a waypoint. This happens only on your PC.
::

## World map

![Fullscreen world map with the waypoint list open](/shots/client/world-map.png)

Press :kbd{value="M"} to open the fullscreen world map. Everything you have explored stays on it – saved per world, server and dimension on your PC. If another map mod already uses M, the TRS key starts unbound instead of clashing.

- **Drag** to move; after letting go, the map glides on for a moment.
- **Mouse wheel** zooms smoothly towards the mouse pointer.
- **Right-click** opens a menu: **Create waypoint here**, **Edit**, **Hide**, **Delete**, **Center here**, **Follow me** and **Share with friends**.
- **Center on me** jumps back to your position; the bottom bar shows the coordinates under the cursor.
- The **level button** switches between surface, cave levels and indoor level.

### Waypoint list

The pin button opens a list of all waypoints of this world: search by name or coordinate, filter by dimension, see the distance (Nether and Overworld converted ×8) and turn each one on or off. Click a waypoint to center the map on it, the pencil edits it, **+** adds one in the middle of the map.

### Other dimensions

Click the dimension name at the top to look at the Nether, the End or other dimensions of this world you have mapped – without going there. In the Nether and the Overworld the bottom bar also shows the matching coordinates of the other side (×8 or ÷8), handy for portals.

### Export as image

The image button saves **Whole explored map** or **Visible area only** as a PNG in your `screenshots` folder. Unexplored parts stay transparent, very large maps are scaled down to the export size (default 8192 pixels). The export runs in the background and a chat message tells you the file name.

### World map settings

Show waypoints, players, hostile mobs and animals, chunk grid when zoomed in, smooth zoom, keep gliding after dragging, view other dimensions, matching Nether/Overworld coordinates, export size and **Map storage on disk** (default 256 MB – the oldest areas are removed first).

## Share waypoints with friends

With the TRS online features you can send a waypoint – or where you stand – to a friend or group in the [social chat](/en/client/social-in-game). It arrives as a card with name, coordinates, dimension and server. Your friend can **Save** it as their own waypoint (on the same server or in the same world) or **Show** it on the map.

## Version notes

::note
The minimap and the world map work in every version from 1.7.10 to 26.3. On 1.7.10 and 1.13.2 there is no **Waypoints** module – create and edit waypoints on the world map with a right-click; the death point is set there too. Sharing waypoints needs the TRS online features and is not available on 1.7.10 and 1.13.2.
::
