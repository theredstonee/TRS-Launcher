## Screenshot preview

After :kbd{value="F2"} a small preview of your screenshot slides in at the top right for a few seconds.

![Screenshot preview with its four buttons](/shots/client/screenshot-toast.png)

::steps
### Open the chat or your inventory
The mouse is free there, so you can use the preview. While your mouse is on it, it stays.

### Pick an action
Hover the preview and four buttons appear:
- **Edit** – opens the screenshot editor,
- **Add to favourites** (the heart),
- **Copy picture** – straight to the clipboard, paste it anywhere with :kbd{value="Ctrl"} + :kbd{value="V"},
- **Send to friends** – pick one or more friends or chats; the picture goes out as a normal chat image.
::

The chat message "Screenshot saved:" has **[Edit]**, **[Copy link]** and **[Copy picture]**, and hovering it shows the picture. **Copy link** uploads the screenshot and copies a share link that works for 30 days.

The module **Screenshot Tools** (category **Other**) has the settings: **Preview after a screenshot**, **Preview duration** (3 – 15 s, default 5), **Actions in the chat message** and **Replace Essential's screenshot preview**.

::note
If Essential is installed, its screenshot popup is replaced by the TRS preview. Turn **Replace Essential's screenshot preview** off to get Essential's preview back.
::

## Screenshot editor

![The screenshot editor with crop handles and drawing tools](/shots/client/screenshot-editor.png)

- **Crop** with handles on the corners and edges, **Rotate 90°**
- **Arrow**, **Frame**, **Text** and **Pen**
- **Pixelate** – hide names or coordinates, great for streamers
- colour and size (thin, medium, thick), **Undo** :kbd{value="Z"} and **Redo** :kbd{value="Y"}

**Save copy** always creates "Copy of …" next to the original – your original screenshot is never changed. **Share** sends it to friends, copies the picture or copies a link.

Favourites show up in their own tab in **Clips & Images**.

## Clips and recording

Clips are recorded by the TRS Launcher – the game window with system sound, the microphone only if you turned it on in the launcher. Nothing leaves your PC.

::steps
### Turn clips on
In the launcher under **Settings → Clips**. Or press :kbd{value="F9"} in game while clips are off, then press it a second time – the launcher switches clips on and starts recording.

### Save a clip
:kbd{value="F9"} saves the last seconds (the length is set in the launcher).

### Record
:kbd{value="F10"} starts and stops a normal recording. A small HUD element shows a red dot and the time while recording and "Clip saved" afterwards; move it in the HUD editor.
::

::warning
Clips only work when the game was started from the TRS Launcher. Started from another launcher, the keys only show a hint.
::

### Clips & Images

Open **Clips & Images** from the TRS menu, the pause menu or the title screen: all your clips and screenshots as tiles. Look at screenshots in full size, start or stop a recording, save a clip, open the folder or delete files. A clip opens a small animated preview with play/pause and a timeline, and **Open in launcher** plays it in the launcher.

## Panorama screenshots

The module **Panorama Screenshots** (category **World**) records a 360° panorama:

::steps
### Take it
Click **Take panorama now** on the module page, or bind the key **Take panorama** in the Minecraft controls (unbound by default). The HUD is hidden while recording.

### Find it
The pictures are saved in `screenshots/panorama`. A message offers to open the folder.

### Share it
**Share** on the module page uploads the 360° picture and copies the link.
::

**Save as**: **6 cube images + 360° image** (default), only **6 cube images** (like the title screen panorama, ready for a resource pack) or only the **360° image**, which you can view in any panorama viewer.

## Version notes

::note
- The screenshot preview, editor, favourites and **Copy picture** work in every version. On 1.7.10 and 1.13.2 there are no links, no sending and no chat actions.
- Copying pictures on Linux needs `wl-clipboard` or `xclip`.
- Panoramas work on 1.8.9 – 1.12.2 and from 1.17 on.
- Clips work in every version when the game was started from the TRS Launcher on Windows.
::
