Every world and every server has its own notebook. Open it with **Notes** in the TRS menu or with the key **Open notes** (unbound by default – set it in the Minecraft controls or on the **World Notes** page).

![The notes of a world with a checklist](/shots/client/notes.png)

## Write a note

::steps
### Join the world or server
Notes belong to the world you are in. Notes of other worlds are under **Other worlds**.

### Create the note
Click **New note** and write a title and text. A world can have up to 200 notes.

### Search
The search box finds notes of the world you are in.
::

## Checklists

Start a line with `[ ]` – or press **Checklist** – and tick the boxes right in the note. :kbd{value="Enter"} continues the list. The list shows how many items are done.

```text
Base plans
[x] Iron farm
[ ] Villager trading hall
[ ] Nether portal x: 120, y: 70, z: -48
```

## Clickable coordinates

Coordinates like `x: 100, y: 64, z: -20` become links. **My position** inserts where you are standing. Click a coordinate to:

- **Show on world map**,
- set a **Temporary waypoint** – it disappears when you get there,
- **Save as waypoint**.

## Pin a note to the HUD

The pin button shows a note – with its checklist – on screen while you are in that world. Move and resize it in the HUD editor like every other HUD element; the module **Pinned Note** has the settings **Lines** and **Width**.

![A pinned note in the HUD](/shots/client/notes-hud.png)

## Sync

Notes are saved in `config/trsclient/notes` and synced with your TRS account note by note:

- the newest change of a note wins,
- deleted notes are deleted on all your PCs,
- changes from another PC arrive within seconds.

Only you can read your notes. To keep them on this PC only, switch off **Sync with TRS account** on the **World Notes** page.

## Version notes

::note
World notes work in every version. On 1.7.10 and 1.13.2 they stay on this PC (no sync).
::
