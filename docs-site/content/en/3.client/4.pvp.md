The PvP modules sit in the category **PvP** of the TRS menu. The quickest start is the **PvP** module pack under **Presets**.

::note
All PvP modules only change what you see. Your reach, hitboxes and attacks stay exactly as in vanilla, and nothing clicks or attacks for you. The only thing the client ever sends by itself is **Auto GG**, which is off until you turn it on.
::

![PvP HUD with keystrokes, CPS, armour and item counter](/shots/client/pvp-hud.png)

## Displays

| Module | What it shows | Settings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Keystrokes** | WASD, mouse buttons and space bar | CPS below mouse buttons, show space bar |
| **CPS** | Clicks per second (left \| right) | – |
| **Armor** | Worn armour with durability, optionally the held item | Durability in percent, **Orientation** vertical or horizontal |
| **Reach** | Distance of your last hit | Decimal places, display time |
| **Combo** | Hits in a row – ends when you get hit or stop hitting | Also show best combo, combo ends after (s) |
| **Speed** | Blocks per second | Count height changes |
| **Item Counter** | Arrows, totems, healing and splash potions, golden apples, ender pearls and blocks in your inventory | Each counter on/off, hide empty counters, orientation, **Totem pops of opponents** (from 1.11) |

### Ping display

The **Ping** module shows the real round-trip time to the server, the jitter (±) and a small history graph. Optionally it adds the server's TPS (an estimate) and timeouts, and warns you about ping spikes.

::tabs
:::tabs-item{label="1.20.2 and newer"}
The TRS Client measures the ping itself with one ping request every 2 seconds (adjustable from 1 to 5 seconds) – the same request the F3 network graph sends.
:::
:::tabs-item{label="Older versions"}
The display shows the value the server reports in the player list and marks it "server".
:::
::

## Crosshair and hit effects

- **Crosshair** – your own crosshair: cross, cross with dot, dot, T shape, circle or circle with dot, with colour, size, gap, thickness, dark outline and the attack cooldown. The button **Edit crosshair** opens a live editor.
- **Hit Color** – the colour mobs and players flash in when hit, with opacity.
- **Hit Feedback** – a hit marker at the crosshair when your hit lands, more critical and sharpness particles (1× to 5×) and an optional hit sound.
- **Block Outline** – colour, opacity and width of the outline around the block you look at.
- **Hitboxes** – shows the hitboxes of mobs like F3+B, in a colour of your choice.

![The crosshair editor with a live preview](/shots/client/crosshair-editor.png)

## Hand and camera

- **1.7 Animations** – the hand does not dip during the attack cooldown, and it swings while you use an item (visual only).
- **Low Fire** – pulls the flames at the edge of the screen down while you are burning.
- **No Hurt Cam** – the camera no longer tilts when you take damage.

## Shield Position

Holds your shield further to the side and lower in first person, so you see more of the fight. While blocking it switches smoothly to its own flatter pose.

::steps
### Turn it on
TRS menu → **PvP** → **Shield Position**.

### Pick a preset
**Side**, **Low** or **Vanilla** – or set position, rotation and size yourself for **Normal** and **Blocking** (the preset then shows **Custom**).

### Check the preview
The live preview on the settings page shows how much of the screen the shield covers.

### Optional: see-through while blocking
**See-through while blocking** with an adjustable opacity.
::

Third person stays unchanged, and shields from other mods work too.

## Auto GG

Sends your message (default "gg") exactly once when a round ends – 0.5 to 2 seconds later and at most every 10 seconds. It knows the end-of-round messages of Hypixel, Minemen and PvP.Land, and you can add your own triggers. **Off by default** – only use it where it is allowed.

## Version notes

::note
- **Hit Color** is not available on 1.7.10, 1.8.9 – 1.12.2, 1.13.2 and Forge 1.14.4.
- **Low Fire** is not available on 1.8.9 – 1.12.2.
- **Shield Position** works from 1.10.2 (Forge) and 1.14.4 (Fabric) up to 26.3, not on 1.13.2 and Forge 1.14.4.
- **Item Counter** and **Hit Feedback** are not available on 1.7.10 and 1.13.2; Hit Feedback also not on Forge 1.14.4.
- On Forge 1.14.4, **Reach**, **Combo**, **Low Fire**, **Block Outline**, **No Hurt Cam** and **Auto GG** are missing.
::
