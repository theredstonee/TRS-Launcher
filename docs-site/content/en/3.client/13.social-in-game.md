**Social** brings your TRS friends into the game: chat, friends list and friends' worlds – live, without reloading. It uses the TRS online features, so the TRS services must be on in the launcher and you need a Microsoft account.

Open it from the title screen, the TRS menu, the pause menu or with the key **Open social** (unbound by default). It has the tabs **Chat**, **Friends** and **Worlds**.

![The Social screen with a chat](/shots/client/social-chat.png)

## Chat

- Write to friends and **groups**. Create a group from your friends, rename it, add or remove members.
- **Reply** to a message, copy it, **edit** or **delete** your own, mark a chat as unread and **react**.
- See when someone is typing or has read your message. Both can be turned off in **Chat settings** – it works both ways.
- **Select pictures** sends up to 10 screenshots of this instance at once. Click a picture to view it large.
- **Invite friends to the server you are on**: they see an invite card with the server icon and player count and join with one click. Before you leave your own world or server you are asked.
- Waypoint cards: see [Share waypoints](/en/client/minimap-and-world-map#share-waypoints-with-friends).

::note
Text from others is always shown without colour or format codes, and links only open after you confirm them.
::

## Friends

The **Friends** tab shows your friends with their Minecraft faces, who is online and what they play, next to **Requests** (including cape offers) and **Blocked** players.

- **Add** a friend by Minecraft name or UUID.
- Accept or decline requests.
- Remove or **Block** a player – blocked players can't find you or send requests.

## Notifications

New messages, server invites, friend requests, cape offers and friends coming online appear as small notifications for a few seconds – above every menu, and out of the way of Minecraft's own pop-ups.

Press :kbd{value="Y"} (**Quick reply / join**) to answer the newest message or join an invite without leaving the game. In the quick reply, :kbd{value="Enter"} sends, :kbd{value="Shift"} + :kbd{value="Enter"} starts a new line and :kbd{value="Esc"} closes.

Settings in the TRS menu under **Social**:

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | on |
| Position | Top right |
| Duration | 5 s (3 – 10 s) |
| Sound | on |
| Do not disturb | off |
| Quiet in fullscreen | off |
| New messages / Server invites / Friend requests and cape offers / Friends coming online | on |

::tip
While a game with the TRS Client is running, the launcher stays quiet about these messages – you only get them once, in the game.
::

## Report and stay safe

Report messages, pictures, players and groups with a reason (insult or hate, spam, inappropriate, scam or phishing, harassment, other) and an optional note. The team sees the reported content and the messages around it, and you get feedback when it has been looked at.

If the team muted you in chat, the chat shows it clearly with the end date. Your sanctions and appeals are listed in Social as well.

## Version notes

::note
Social is available from Minecraft 1.8.9 on (not on 1.7.10 and 1.13.2). Worlds of friends are explained on [World hosting](/en/client/world-hosting).
::
