## Emote wheel

Hold :kbd{value="R"}, point at an emote with the mouse and release the key to play it. Other players with the TRS Client see it too. If you only tap the key, the wheel stays open – click an emote, :kbd{value="Esc"} closes it.

![The emote wheel](/shots/client/emote-wheel.png)

Emotes: Wave, Clap, Cheer, Bow, Facepalm, Shrug, Thumbs up, Dance, Salute, Air guitar, Redstone dance and Party. Emotes you haven't unlocked are marked **Locked** – some are unlocked with a code in the launcher, **Party** is the reward for having 10 friends. Walking, sneaking or attacking ends an emote.

Settings of the module **Emotes**: **Camera during your emote** (Unchanged, Third person behind, Third person front) and **Show emotes of other players**.

::note
Emotes need the TRS online features (see [Overview](/en/client/overview#trs-online-features)). If you already used G for the emote wheel, it stays on G – you can pick any key in the Minecraft controls under **Emote wheel (hold)**.
::

## Wardrobe

Open the wardrobe with the **Wardrobe** button under your figure on the title screen, from the TRS menu, from the pause menu or with the key **Open wardrobe** (unbound by default). It has four tabs, all with a big 3D preview:

![The wardrobe with skins, favourites and 3D preview](/shots/client/wardrobe.png)

- **Skins** – the **Current** card shows the skin your account wears right now. Below are your favourites and your library. **Add skin** takes a file, a link, a player name or **Paint a new skin**. **Apply** makes it your Minecraft skin right away – others see it after rejoining.
- **Outfits** – **Save current look** stores skin and cape together; **Put on** wears an outfit.
- **Capes** – your Minecraft capes and your TRS capes; **No cape** takes it off. Approved capes you uploaded can be offered to a friend with **Share with a friend**.
- **Emotes** – arrange your emote wheel: pick a slot, then an emote. Right-click empties a slot; an empty wheel shows all emotes.

::tip
With the TRS services on, your skins, favourites, outfits and emote wheel are the same as in the launcher and on every PC. Otherwise everything stays on this PC. Applying skins and Minecraft capes needs a Microsoft account.
::

### Skin editor

Paint straight onto the 3D figure or the flat skin.

| Tool | Key |
| --- | --- |
| Brush | :kbd{value="B"} |
| Eraser | :kbd{value="E"} |
| Eyedropper | :kbd{value="I"} |
| Fill | :kbd{value="G"} |
| Mirror left/right | :kbd{value="M"} |
| Undo / Redo | :kbd{value="Z"} / :kbd{value="Y"} |

You can switch between base layer and outer layer, pick colours from a palette with recent colours or as hex, and start from **Blank (classic)**, **Blank (slim)** or your **Current skin**. Right-drag turns the figure.

![The skin editor](/shots/client/wardrobe-editor.png)

## TRS capes and badges

The module **TRS Online Features** shows TRS capes and the TRS badge – a small glowing redstone dust pile in front of the names of players who play with the TRS Client right now.

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Badge in the tab list | on |
| Badge above names | on |
| Show TRS capes | on |
| Show TRS cosmetics (hats) | on |
| Sync with TRS account | on |

TRS capes are chosen in the wardrobe or in the launcher; you upload your own capes in the launcher.

## Cape physics

The module **Cape Physics** makes capes (Mojang, OptiFine, TRS) move like cloth: they swing when you walk, turn, jump and sneak. A turning live preview of your own player shows every change.

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Style | Smooth, Blocky |
| Wind | Off, Waves, Gusts – plus wind strength |
| Movement | Vanilla, Swinging, Dungeons (calm) |
| Gravity, Lift when moving, Stiffness | 0 – 200 % |
| Detail | High, Medium, Low (faster) |
| For | Only my own, All players |

## Switch accounts in the game

Open **Accounts** in the TRS menu, the pause menu or the title screen bar and click **Switch** – no restart needed. Leave the world first.

::tabs
:::tabs-item{label="Started from the TRS Launcher"}
All your launcher accounts are there. **Add account** opens the Microsoft sign-in through the launcher and saves the new account in the launcher too.
:::
:::tabs-item{label="Started from another launcher"}
Sign in with Microsoft in the game – in the browser or with a code on microsoft.com/link. The accounts are kept per game folder.
:::
::

## Version notes

::note
- Emotes are not available on 1.7.10, 1.13.2 and Forge 1.14.4.
- TRS capes and badges are not shown on 1.7.10, 1.13.2 and Forge 1.14.4.
- Cape physics is not available on 1.7.10, 1.13.2 and 1.14.4.
- The wardrobe and account switching work in every version.
::
