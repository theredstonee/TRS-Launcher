To play, you sign in with the **Microsoft account you bought Minecraft: Java Edition with**. You enter your password only on Microsoft's own page in your browser – the launcher never sees it.

::note
There is no offline or "cracked" mode. Without a signed-in account with a Minecraft license the game doesn't start.
::

## Add an account

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the accounts
Click **Sign in** at the bottom of the left sidebar, or open the account menu at the top right of the title bar and choose **Add account**.

### Sign in with Microsoft
Click **Add account** (or **Sign in with Microsoft**). Your browser opens Microsoft's sign-in page. Sign in there – the launcher continues by itself as soon as you're done.

### Ready to play
The account shows up in the list with its skin head. The active account is marked **Active** and is used when you launch the game.
::

![The accounts page with two signed-in Microsoft accounts](/shots/getting-started/accounts.png)

### Sign in with a code

If the browser sign-in doesn't work on your PC, click **Sign in with code**. The launcher shows a short code and opens Microsoft's page – enter the code there. Click the code to copy it.

## Several accounts

You can add as many Microsoft accounts as you like.

- **Switch:** open the account menu at the top right of the title bar and click **Switch** next to another account. On the accounts page the same button is called **Use**.
- **Manage:** **Manage accounts** in the account menu opens the accounts page.
- **Sign out:** the sign-out button next to an account removes it from the launcher. Your instances and worlds stay.

![The account menu in the title bar](/shots/getting-started/account-menu.png)

::tip
With the TRS Client you can even switch accounts inside the game, without restarting it.
::

## Where your sign-in is stored

Sign-in tokens stay on your PC, encrypted: on Windows with Windows' own encryption for your user, on Linux with a key from your system keyring. They are never sent to the TRS server or anyone else. When a sign-in expires, the launcher renews it by itself.

## Sign in on the TRS website

On [trs-launcher.theredstonee.de](https://trs-launcher.theredstonee.de) you can sign in with your launcher instead of typing a password:

::steps{level="3"}
### Choose "TRS Launcher" on the website
The launcher opens and shows a code, the website and the browser that asks.

### Compare the code
Check that your browser shows exactly the same code. You can pick another of your accounts.

### Confirm
Click **Confirm** – you are signed in on the website. Nothing is confirmed without your click.
::

On another PC, type the code from the website under **Settings → Privacy → Sign in on the website** (or search for it with :kbd{value="Ctrl"} :kbd{value="K"}). Signing in on the website uses the optional TRS services – see [Settings](/en/launcher/settings#privacy).

::warning
Only confirm a code if you just started the sign-in on the website yourself. Never confirm a code someone sent you.
::
