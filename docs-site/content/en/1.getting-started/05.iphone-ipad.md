TRS Launcher runs on iPhone and iPad too. It isn't in the App Store – you **sideload** it with **AltStore** or **SideStore**, which sign the app with your own Apple ID and keep it up to date from our source.

| You need | |
|---|---|
| iOS / iPadOS | **15 or newer** |
| Sideloading | **AltStore** or **SideStore** (or TrollStore, if your iOS version supports it) |
| Apple ID | A free one is enough – the app then has to be refreshed every 7 days |
| Account | A Microsoft account that **owns Minecraft: Java Edition** |
| To play | **JIT** – see [Enable JIT](#enable-jit) |

::note
**Without JIT** the app still works as a companion: friends and chat, wardrobe and capes, remote control of the launcher on your PC and notifications. Only **playing Minecraft** needs JIT.
::

## Install

![Illustration of the three steps in AltStore or SideStore: open Sources, paste the source URL, install TRS Launcher](/shots/getting-started/ios-altstore-steps.png)

::steps{level="3"}
### Install AltStore or SideStore
If you don't have one yet, set up **AltStore** (with AltServer on a Windows PC or Mac) or **SideStore** (refreshes on the device itself) by following the official guide of that app. It's the hardest step – after that everything happens on the phone.

### Add the TRS source
On your iPhone or iPad, open the [download page](https://trs-launcher.theredstonee.de/download#ios) and tap **Add to AltStore** or **Add to SideStore**. Or add it by hand: in AltStore/SideStore open **Sources**, tap **+** and paste this URL:

```text
https://github.com/theredstonee/TRS-Launcher/releases/download/mobile/altstore.json
```

### Install TRS Launcher
Open the source **TRS Launcher**, tap the app and then **Free** / **Install**. AltStore or SideStore signs it with your Apple ID and installs it. Keep the app open until it's done.

### Open the app
TRS Launcher now shows up on your home screen. On iOS 16 and newer, iOS may ask you to turn on **Developer Mode** first (**Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode**); the phone restarts once.
::

::tip
You can also download the IPA file directly from the [download page](https://trs-launcher.theredstonee.de/download#ios) and install it with **My Apps → +** in AltStore/SideStore. With the source, though, you get updates automatically.
::

## Enable JIT

Minecraft runs on Java, and Java needs **JIT** (just-in-time compilation) to be fast enough. iOS only allows JIT for sideloaded apps in special ways. Pick the one that fits your setup:

::tabs
:::tabs-item{label="SideStore + StikDebug" icon="i-lucide-smartphone"}
For **iOS 17.4 and newer**, completely on the device:

1. Install **StikDebug** (for example from SideStore) and set it up once as its own guide describes (pairing file).
2. Open **StikDebug** and choose **TRS Launcher** from the list – StikDebug starts the app with JIT enabled.
3. Start your instance in TRS Launcher.

Do this every time you want to play; StikDebug has to be used again after the app was closed.
:::

:::tabs-item{label="AltStore + AltServer" icon="i-lucide-monitor"}
With **AltServer** on your PC or Mac on the same Wi-Fi (or connected by cable):

1. Make sure AltServer is running.
2. In AltStore open **My Apps**, press and hold **TRS Launcher** and choose **Enable JIT** – or use **Enable JIT** in AltServer's menu on the computer (AltJIT).
3. TRS Launcher opens with JIT; start your instance.

Repeat this whenever the app was closed completely.
:::

:::tabs-item{label="TrollStore" icon="i-lucide-zap"}
If your iOS version supports **TrollStore**, install the IPA with TrollStore. JIT is then enabled **automatically** – there is nothing else to do, and the app doesn't expire after 7 days.
:::
::

::warning
Re-signing with AltStore/SideStore can remove the **increased memory** permission of the app. The game then gets less memory than on a comparable Android phone: prefer Vanilla or small modpacks, keep the render distance low and close other apps.
::

## First start and sign-in

::steps{level="3"}
### Language and welcome
Pick your language; the short setup works like on the PC.

### Sign in with Microsoft
Tap **Sign in**. The app shows a short **code** and opens Microsoft's page. Enter the code there and sign in with the account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition. Your password only goes to Microsoft.

### Play
Create an instance ([Your first instance](/en/getting-started/first-instance)), make sure JIT is on and tap **Play**. The game files come from Mojang, the TRS Client is loaded by itself and the touch controls are the same as on Android – see [Touch controls](/en/getting-started/android#touch-controls).
::

## Notifications

Under **More → Settings → Notifications** you switch on notifications for chat messages, friend requests and invitations (the TRS online features must be on). iOS has no ntfy: the app **checks in the background** for new messages and shows them as notifications. iOS decides how often that happens, so notifications can come late – especially when the phone is in Low Power Mode or the app wasn't used for a while.

## Updates and the 7-day limit

- **Updates:** when a new version is in our source, AltStore/SideStore shows an update – tap **Update**. Your instances and sign-in stay.
- **Refresh every 7 days:** apps signed with a **free Apple ID** stop opening after 7 days. Open AltStore or SideStore regularly and tap **Refresh All** (AltStore needs AltServer reachable for that, SideStore does it on the device). If the app already expired, refreshing it brings it back – nothing is lost.
- A free Apple ID allows only **3 sideloaded apps** at the same time, and AltStore/SideStore counts as one of them.

## Problems

### The game closes right after starting
Almost always **JIT is not active**. Start TRS Launcher again through StikDebug, AltStore/AltServer or TrollStore as described above, then start the instance. If it still crashes, open the instance, go to the log and tap **Share log** – it uploads the log to mclo.gs with your username and secrets removed. The [crash helper](/en/help/crash-helper) explains many crashes in plain words.

### Minecraft 26.3 doesn't start
Minecraft **26.3** is not supported on iPhone and iPad yet. Use **26.2 or older** for now.

### The game runs out of memory
See the memory warning above: smaller modpacks, lower render distance, close other apps.

### "Unable to verify app" or the app doesn't open any more
The 7 days are over. Open AltStore/SideStore and refresh the app.

### Adding the source doesn't work
Make sure you copied the full URL, including `https://` and `altstore.json`. If the buttons on the download page do nothing, AltStore/SideStore isn't installed – add the source by hand instead.

### No notifications
Allow notifications for TRS Launcher in **iOS Settings → Notifications**, keep **Background App Refresh** on for the app and open it now and then – iOS checks less often for apps you rarely use.

If nothing helps, [report a bug](/en/help/report-a-bug) and include the log link.
