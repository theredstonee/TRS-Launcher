TRS Launcher keeps itself up to date. New versions are signed, download in the background while you play and install when you restart.

## Update the launcher

::tabs
:::tabs-item{label="Windows / AppImage" icon="i-lucide-monitor"}
1. When a new version is out, the launcher downloads it in the background. The tasks panel shows "Downloading update in the background".
2. As soon as it's ready, a **Restart to update** button appears in the title bar.
3. Click it. You see a short "Updating – restarting in a moment …" screen, then the new version starts. Running games keep running.

If an update fails, the title bar shows why and offers **Retry update** or **Download installer**.
:::

:::tabs-item{label="Linux packages" icon="i-lucide-terminal"}
The .deb, .rpm and AUR packages are updated by your package manager. The launcher only tells you: "Update … available" with a link to the release.

- Arch (AUR): `yay -Syu` or `paru -Syu`
- Debian/Ubuntu: download the new `.deb` and install it with `sudo apt install ./TRS-Launcher_<version>_amd64.deb`
- Fedora/openSUSE: install the new `.rpm` with `dnf` or `zypper`
:::
::

## The TRS Client updates itself

The TRS Client has its own signed update channel and gets fixes without a new launcher version. It updates itself on the next game start. The current TRS Client version is shown at the bottom of the settings.

## What's new

After an update the launcher shows **What's new in …** once, with the notes of the new version (in English and German).

Every update also has a post of its own: click the update card on the home page, the update in **News** (filter **Launcher**) or a version in "What's new". The post shows the update banner, a gallery of screenshots of the new features (click to enlarge, browse with the arrow keys), the release notes and **View on website**.

![The update post with banner and screenshot gallery](/shots/launcher/update-news.png)

::tip
Don't want the update card on the home page? Click **Hide until the next update**.
::
