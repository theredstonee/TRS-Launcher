**Social** in the sidebar brings your friends into the launcher: a friends list with online status, direct messages and groups, pictures, server invites and your friends' open worlds. Everything updates live, and the TRS Client shows the same chats in the game.

## Turn on the TRS services

Friends and chat run over the optional **TRS services**. They are off until you agree.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open Social
Click **Social** in the sidebar. As long as the services are off, you see "TRS services are turned off".

### Read what's stored
Click **Learn more & turn on**. The dialog explains what the TRS server stores (for example your Minecraft UUID and name, friends and chat messages).

### Agree
Click **Use TRS services**. The launcher signs in to the TRS server with your Minecraft account – like joining a Minecraft server. The server never gets your password or your Minecraft token.
::

::tip
You can turn the services off at any time under **Settings → Privacy** and delete all your TRS data there.
::

## Friends

The **Friends** tab shows your **Friend list**, **Friend requests** and **Blocked players** side by side.

- **Add friend:** enter the player's Minecraft name and click **Send request**. Your friend needs to have used the TRS Launcher with TRS services at least once.
- **Requests:** **Accept** or **Decline** requests to you, **Withdraw** your own. Cape offers from friends show up here too.
- The list shows who is online, what they are playing and when they were last online. Click a friend to see their achievements, or send a message.
- **Remove friend** and **Block** are in the conversation menu. Blocked players can't find you, can't send requests and don't see your TRS badge or cape – they aren't told.

## Chat

The **Chat** tab lists your conversations. Click a friend or group and write – :kbd{value="Enter"} sends, :kbd{value="Shift"} + :kbd{value="Enter"} adds a new line.

- **Reply**, **React** with an emoji, copy messages, edit or delete your own, and **Mark as unread**. Right-click a message for all options.
- You see when someone is typing and when your message was **Read**. Both can be turned off in the privacy settings.
- **Mute** a conversation for 1 hour, 8 hours, 1 day or until you unmute it.
- Links only open after you confirm them.

![A chat with pictures and a server invite](/shots/launcher/social-chat.png)

### Groups

**Create group**, name it and add friends. As the owner you can rename the group, add or remove members, **Make owner** of someone else or **Delete group**. Members can **Leave group** at any time. New members see messages from the moment they join.

### Pictures

Click **Pictures** in the message box to send up to 10 pictures at once: screenshots of all your instances, **Favourites** or **Uploads** from your PC. You can also drag pictures into the window or paste them with :kbd{value="Ctrl"} :kbd{value="V"}.

### Server invites

**Invite to a server** picks one of your servers or an address. Your friends see a card with the server's icon and player count. **Join** asks which instance to use (matching versions first), starts it and connects right away.

## Worlds

When a friend opens their singleplayer world in the TRS Client (pause menu → **Host world**), it shows up live in the **Worlds** tab with version, game mode and player count.

::steps{level="3"}
### Join or ask
Invited? Click **Join**. Not invited? Click **Ask to join** – or **Join with a code** if you got one like `K7Q-M2X`. The host decides whether you get in.

### Pick the instance
The launcher chooses an instance with the same Minecraft version and loader (you choose if there are several). If none fits, **Create and join** makes one with the TRS Client.

### Play
The game starts and joins the world by itself – no address to type, no port forwarding.
::

![Social → Worlds with a friend's open world](/shots/launcher/social-worlds.png)

If the world shares mods, the launcher shows them first and lets you **Create a new instance**, add them to a copy of an existing instance (your original stays untouched) or **Join without mods** when nothing required is missing. Mods from Modrinth and CurseForge are downloaded from their official source. Mods sent directly by the host come with a warning and are only installed after you tick **I trust this host**.

::note
With a direct connection you and the other players see each other's IP address. If that doesn't work, the game data runs through the TRS relay.
::

## Notifications

Small pop-ups tell you about new messages (with **Reply** right in the notification), server and world invites, friend requests, cape offers and friends coming online. When the launcher is in the background, Windows shows them too. While you play with the TRS Client, the launcher stays quiet – the game shows them instead.

Choose position, duration, sound, **Do not disturb** and which kinds you want under **Settings → Notifications**.

## Report and stay safe

- **Report** messages, pictures, players or groups with a reason and an optional note. The team sees the reported content and a few messages around it; the reported player doesn't learn who reported them.
- **Settings → Privacy → My reports** shows what happened to your reports.
- If the team gives you a sanction, **Settings → Privacy → My sanctions** shows what it blocks, when it ends and why – and you can appeal each one once.

Who can see your online status and server is set under [Settings → Privacy](/en/launcher/settings#privacy).
