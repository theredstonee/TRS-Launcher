**Skins** in the sidebar is your wardrobe: collect skins, try them on in a 3D preview, pick your Mojang cape and wear TRS capes that everyone with the TRS Client can see.

![The Skins page with the 3D preview and your skin collection](/shots/launcher/skins.png)

## Change your skin

::steps{level="3"}
### Try it on
Click a skin under **My skins** to see it on the 3D figure. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, and switch between **Walk**, **Idle** and **Stand**.

### Pick model and cape
Choose the **Model** – **Classic** (4-pixel arms, Steve) or **Slim** (3-pixel arms, Alex) – and one of your **Mojang capes**.

### Apply
Nothing changes on your account until you click **Apply**. **Discard** throws your changes away.
::

The launcher sends the change to Mojang in the background. If Mojang slows down requests, it waits and applies the change by itself – you see a short note.

## Add skins

**Add skin** opens a menu:

- **Choose files** – one or more PNG files (64×64, or 64×32 for old skins). You can also drag PNG files into the window.
- **By link** – a public https link to a PNG image.
- **By player name** – copies the skin a player is wearing right now.
- **From other launchers** – skins you saved in the official Minecraft Launcher, Prism Launcher, the Modrinth App or ATLauncher.

The launcher recognises slim arms by itself; for a single skin you can still change the name and model. **Save current skin** adds the skin your account is wearing to your collection. You can rename and delete skins in your collection at any time.

::tip
With the TRS services and **Sync with TRS account** on, "My skins" is the same on all your PCs – and in the TRS Client's wardrobe in the game.
::

## TRS capes

TRS capes are separate from your Mojang capes: everyone who plays with the TRS Client sees them (some are animated). They need the optional TRS services.

- Click a cape to try it on, then **Put on** – or **Take off**.
- Locked capes can be tried on anyway; some can be unlocked with a code, others come from [achievements](/en/launcher/achievements).
- Once you've unlocked a **head cosmetic**, it's listed below the capes and put on the same way.

### Redeem a code

Click **Redeem code** in the TRS capes section and enter the code (20 characters, for example `7K3QF-M2XPA-9RTVB-C4HJN` – case and hyphens don't matter). The unlocked cape or cosmetic appears in your collection right away.

### Upload your own cape

::steps{level="3"}
### Choose images
**Upload your own cape** → **Choose files**: any PNG, JPEG or WebP image, an animated GIF, several images as frames, a finished cape texture or sprite sheet, or a TRS Studio export.

### Pick the part
Drag and zoom to choose the part that goes on the cape and watch it live on the player. For animations, choose the speed.

### Upload
Up to 512×256 pixels per frame, up to 16 frames, at most 5 MB. Click **Upload**.
::

The team reviews every cape – until then only you can see it. Only upload your own images or ones you're allowed to use.

![Uploading your own cape with the live preview](/shots/launcher/cape-upload.png)

### Share a cape with friends

Once your own cape is approved, **Share with a friend** offers it to a friend, who can accept or decline. Friends may pass it on to their friends – up to 20 players per cape. **Shared** shows who has it, and you can take it back at any time; everyone they passed it on to loses it too.

## Emotes

Emotes and the emote wheel live in the TRS Client's wardrobe in the game. Rewards like the **Party** emote show up there automatically.
