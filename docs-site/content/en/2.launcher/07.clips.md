TRS Launcher can record your game like a replay tool: **F9** saves the last seconds as a clip, **F10** starts and stops a normal recording. All clips and screenshots of all instances are collected in two galleries.

::note
Clip recording is available on **Windows** only. The screenshot gallery works on Windows and Linux.
::

## Turn clips on

Clips are off by default.

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the clip settings
**Settings → Clips & recording** – or **Turn on** on the **Clips** page.

### Switch on "Clips & recording"
The launcher downloads the recorder (FFmpeg) once and checks it by checksum.

### Adjust if you like
**Clip length** (how far back a clip goes), **Resolution**, **Frame rate**, **Quality** and the **Encoder** (Automatic uses your NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics card and falls back to software). Under **Sound**: **System sound** is on, **Microphone** is off by default. **Storage limit** moves the oldest clips to the recycle bin when the folder gets too big.
::

::tip
Everything stays on your PC: no upload, no account. The recorder only runs while a game from this launcher is open, and it records only the game window.
::

## Record in the game

While an instance started from the launcher is running:

- :kbd{value="F9"} saves the last seconds as a clip.
- :kbd{value="F10"} starts and stops a recording.

The keys come with the TRS Client – change them in the Minecraft controls under "TRS Client". The **Clips** page also has **Save clip**, **Start recording** and **Stop recording** for running games.

::tip
If clips are off and you press F9 in the game, the TRS Client tells you – press F9 (or F10) a second time and the launcher switches clips on and starts recording.
::

## The clip gallery

**Clips** in the sidebar shows all your clips as tiles with a preview picture, length and date. Search by name, filter by instance and sort by date, length, size or name. Hover over a tile for a moving preview. Every instance also has its own **Clips** tab.

![The clip gallery with preview tiles](/shots/launcher/clips-gallery.png)

### The player

Click a clip to play it. The timeline shows a small preview while you hover over it.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| :kbd{value="Space"} or :kbd{value="K"} | Play / pause |
| :kbd{value="←"} / :kbd{value="→"} | 5 seconds back / forward |
| :kbd{value="J"} / :kbd{value="L"} | 10 seconds back / forward |
| :kbd{value=","} / :kbd{value="."} | Single frame |
| :kbd{value="F"} | Full screen |
| :kbd{value="M"} | Mute |
| :kbd{value="Alt"} + :kbd{value="←"} / :kbd{value="→"} | Previous / next clip |

If a clip can't be played in the launcher, it offers to open it in your system's video player.

### Trim a clip

::steps{level="4"}
#### Click "Trim"
Set **Start** and **End** on the timeline – or press :kbd{value="I"} and :kbd{value="O"} at the current position.

#### Check the selection
**Preview** plays only the selection. Under **How to cut** you choose **Automatic**, **Fast** or **Exact**.

#### Save
**Save as new clip** – the original always stays unchanged.
::

![Trimming a clip in the clip player](/shots/launcher/clip-trim.png)

**Fast** copies without re-encoding in an instant (the start moves to the previous keyframe). **Exact** re-encodes frame-accurately and takes a moment. **Automatic** copies when the start is on a keyframe and cuts exactly otherwise.

### Share a clip

**Save as …** exports a copy, **Copy file** puts the clip on the clipboard so you can paste it into Discord or a folder. You can also **Show in folder**, **Rename** and delete clips (to the recycle bin).

## The screenshot gallery

**Screenshots** in the sidebar collects the screenshots of all instances (take them with :kbd{value="F2"} in the game). Filter by instance and click a picture to see it in full size – browse with the arrow keys, :kbd{value="Esc"} closes.

- **Copy** puts the picture on the clipboard.
- **Show in folder** opens it in your file manager.
- Delete moves it to the recycle bin (on Linux: the trash).
- **Share as link** uploads the picture and copies a public link that works for 30 days – Discord shows a preview. **My shared pictures** lists your links and lets you delete them earlier. This uses the optional TRS services.

![The screenshot gallery with all instances](/shots/launcher/screenshots.png)

::note
Shared pictures are re-encoded without any hidden data, and the web page never shows your name. Anyone can report a picture.
::
