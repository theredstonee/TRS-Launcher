TRS Launcher has its own achievements: for **playtime**, for trying out **launcher** features and for the **community** – plus a few **secret** ones you only see once you've unlocked them. Every achievement earns points and has a rarity, from Common to Legendary. Some come with a reward.

::note
Achievements use the optional TRS services. If they are off, the page asks you to turn them on first – see [Friends & chat](/en/launcher/social#turn-on-the-trs-services).
::

## Open your achievements

- the account menu at the top right of the title bar → **Achievements**
- the trophy in **Social**
- :kbd{value="Ctrl"} :kbd{value="K"} → "Achievements"

The page shows your points and how many achievements you've unlocked, grouped by category, with progress bars (playtime in hours) and unlock dates. **Show** filters **All**, **Unlocked** or **Locked**.

![The achievements page with points, rarities and rewards](/shots/launcher/achievements.png)

## Rewards

Some achievements unlock something for the TRS Client, for example:

- 100 hours played – the **Veteran** cape
- an idea of yours gets implemented – the **Ideengeber** cape
- 10 friends – the **Party** emote

When you unlock an achievement, a notification pops up. If it gives you a cape or a head cosmetic, **Wear now** puts it on right away. For an emote, **Where is it?** tells you where to find it in the TRS Client.

::tip
Don't want these pop-ups? Turn off **Settings → Notifications → Unlocked achievements**.
::

## Your friends' achievements

Click a friend in **Social** to see their points and latest achievements, or open all of them. Secret achievements stay "???" until you have them too.

Want to keep yours private? Turn off **Achievements visible to friends** on the achievements page or under **Settings → Privacy**.
