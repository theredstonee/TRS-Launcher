Already play with another launcher? TRS Launcher finds it and copies your instances over: **worlds, mods, resource and shader packs, settings and key bindings, and your server list**. The Minecraft version and mod loader are detected for you.

::note
Your other launcher stays untouched – its files are only read. Sign-in data of other launchers is never read or copied.
::

## Supported launchers

The launcher looks for these on your PC:

- Minecraft Launcher (the official one)
- CurseForge
- Modrinth App
- Prism Launcher and MultiMC
- ATLauncher
- GDLauncher (new and legacy)
- Lunar Client and Badlion Client
- OneClient
- TLauncher

Anything else – for example another client's game folder – you can pick with **Browse folder …**. Feather Client is recognised but keeps no readable list of profiles, so pick its game folder by hand.

## Import instances

::steps{level="3"}
### Open the import
**Library → Import**, the **Import** tile on the home page, or :kbd{value="Ctrl"} :kbd{value="K"} → "Import from another launcher". The first-start setup offers the same step.

### Pick what you want
Under **Found on this PC** you see every launcher with its installations. Filter by source or search. **Details** shows exactly what comes along: worlds, mods, packs, settings and the server list.

### Import
Click **Import** next to an installation. The copy runs in the background – you can close the dialog. For a folder you picked yourself, the launcher asks you to **check version and mod loader** first, because it can only guess them there.
::

## TRS Client for imported mods

When you import instances with mods, the launcher asks once whether they get the TRS Client – the same choice as for [modpacks](/en/launcher/modpacks#with-or-without-trs-client). You choose once for all and can tick exceptions per instance.

## Good to know

- **Mods stay updatable.** Content from CurseForge, ATLauncher and GDLauncher keeps its origin, so you can update it right away. Files missing from a CurseForge instance are downloaded for you.
- **Lunar Client and Badlion** bring your worlds, packs, settings and your own mods. Their built-in client mods aren't free and stay behind – the TRS Client takes their place.
- **Profiles that share a game folder** bring everything in that folder along – the dialog tells you.
- **Links that point outside** the other launcher's folders are skipped.
- **Never copied:** sign-in data, caches, logs and the other launcher's own files.

::tip
You can also import a single modpack file here: **Modpack file (.mrpack, .zip) …**.
::

## Linux

On Linux the launcher looks in the usual Linux folders – `~/.minecraft`, `~/.local/share` and the Flatpak folders of the official launcher, Prism Launcher and the Modrinth App. Badlion and Feather aren't searched on Linux. Anything else you pick with **Browse folder …**.
